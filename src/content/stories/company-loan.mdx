The business was doing well   customers and revenue were both increasing. But growth created a new problem: the company needed funding to support its next stage, and the owners didn't want to drain their working capital just to fund expansion.

The lending conversation wasn't simply "how much can we lend you?" It was: *"What does the business need the money for, and what structure makes sense?"*

The right finance solution helped the owners focus on what they actually wanted   growing the business.
