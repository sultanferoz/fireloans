The client wasn't borrowing in a simple personal structure   they were using a trust as part of their business and investment arrangements. Lending through a trust can involve more complexity than a standard residential application: the trust structure, income, existing commitments, borrowing entity, supporting documentation and lender requirements all needed to be understood first.

Instead of treating the application like a normal home loan, the structure was reviewed before the numbers were.

The result wasn't simply a loan   it was a lending solution designed around the client's existing structure.
