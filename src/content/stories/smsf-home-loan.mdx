A couple had spent years building their superannuation. As they started thinking about retirement differently, they wanted to explore whether property could form part of their long-term SMSF investment strategy.

But SMSF property lending isn't the same as getting a normal home loan — there are different structures, rules and lending considerations. The first step wasn't finding a property. It was understanding whether the proposed strategy and structure made sense for them.

With appropriate professional guidance and the right lending structure, they were able to explore the possibility of using property as part of their broader retirement strategy.
