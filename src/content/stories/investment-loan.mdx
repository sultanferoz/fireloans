A property investor had already purchased their first investment property. Now they wanted to take the next step. But there was a problem — they assumed that because they already had a mortgage, getting finance for another investment would be extremely difficult.

Instead of looking at the new loan in isolation, their overall financial position was considered. The goal wasn't simply "how much can we borrow?" It was: *"How can we structure the next investment without putting unnecessary pressure on the rest of the portfolio?"*

After reviewing the options, they were able to move forward with a strategy designed around their longer-term property goals.

The lesson? Your first property may be a purchase. Your second can be the beginning of a strategy.
