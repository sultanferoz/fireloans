They had found the block. They had designed the house. They could already imagine the backyard finished. But there was one major step left: financing the construction.

A construction loan isn't simply one payment — funding is generally structured around the construction process and progress of the build, which meant the finance needed to be planned around the project. The clients didn't just want a loan; they wanted to know: *"Will the finance keep up with our build?"*

After working through the requirements and suitable lending options, they could move forward with the construction journey. Months later, the empty block was no longer empty. It was home.
