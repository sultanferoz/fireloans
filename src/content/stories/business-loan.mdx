A business owner found an opportunity to expand   the location was right, the demand was there. But there was one problem: they needed capital, and waiting months to build enough cash reserves could mean losing the opportunity altogether.

The conversation shifted from "can we get a loan?" to: *"What funding does the business actually need, and how will it be used?"*

Once the funding requirements and business position were understood, suitable finance options could be explored.
