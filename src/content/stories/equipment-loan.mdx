A small business had reached a point where manual processes were holding it back. More work was coming in, but they didn't have the equipment needed to handle it efficiently, and buying it outright would put significant pressure on their cash reserves.

So they explored equipment finance. The goal was simple: get the equipment, keep the business running, protect working capital, and use the new capacity to take on more work.

The equipment wasn't the end goal. Growth was.
